# P02-003 B7 Second-round Behavior Causality Matrix

This matrix records the executable B7 runner chain, not registry visibility. Every row is observed
through a real `ACTION_TRACE` sourced by its committed behavior `CLOCK_ADVANCED` event in
`tests/p02-003-b7-runner.test.ts`. The trace test uses a complete legal match and requires the
full frozen 34-row selectable set, direct result-event references, Creation/DefensiveAction Facts,
and a non-neutral shot opportunity-quality input.

All selectable behavior choices use `BEHAVIOR/<selectionOrdinal>`; duration and ordinary-gap values
are deterministic subvalues of the one `SEGMENT_DURATION/0` root. NORMAL and TRANSITION consume an
ordinary-gap subvalue (including a zero gap); LATE_CLOCK prohibits it. `TRANSITIOND` is the forced
singleton at a legal transition entry and uses the existing `TRANSITION/0` root only after its
behavior clock. No row adds a top-level RNG draw kind.
