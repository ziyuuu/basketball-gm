# P01 Scope Snapshot

P01 proves a deterministic, serializable, recoverable three-school-year headless skeleton. Prototype attributes, training values, budget values, and match model A are disposable calibration fixtures.

R1 was limited to event-audit identity integrity. R2 retains that contract and is limited to
budget-ledger time integrity, bounded persisted-week validation, regression coverage, refreshed
replay evidence, and the engine-version boundary required by the corrected state identity. It adds
no P02 gameplay or balance work.

The R2 engineering baseline is now accepted. P01's gameplay fixtures are deliberately reopened as
inputs to the current P01/P02 gameplay baseline; the command, determinism, persistence, time,
audit, and state-integrity guarantees are not reopened.

Formal recruitment, model B, detailed events, production UI, official characters, final art,
balance claims, and all LLM/Agent behavior were excluded from P01 implementation. Model C is no
longer an MVP requirement.
